About StitchSense

An intelligent computer vision system engineered to bridge haute couture tailoring craftsmanship with modern semantic segmentation.

The Challenge

The Hidden Cost of Sewing Defects in Modern Tailoring

In boutique fashion studios and home tailoring businesses, sewing flaws often remain undetected until final customer fittings. A single blunt needle or slightly miscalibrated thread tension dial can chew delicate knit fibers or cause structural seam failure during routine laundering.

Traditional industrial quality control systems rely on massive overhead conveyor cameras designed for high-speed mills. Home tailors and boutique ateliers have lacked an accessible, lightweight assistant that can run on standard workshop laptops without expensive GPU clusters.

Under The Hood

Deep Learning Architecture

📐

SMP U-Net Architecture

U-Net utilizes long skip connections between encoder and decoder feature maps. This design is optimal for sewing defect segmentation because skip connections preserve fine, high-frequency spatial details required to detect thin 0.5mm stitches.

⚡

MobileNetV2 Backbone

By utilizing inverted residual bottlenecks and depthwise separable convolutions, MobileNetV2 drastically reduces compute FLOPs and parameter counts (~2.2M parameters) without sacrificing spatial fidelity on CPU hardware.

⚖️

Compound Loss Formulation

Combines Multi-Class Focal Loss (down-weighting easy background fabric pixels) with Soft Dice Loss (directly optimizing region overlap), overcoming extreme class imbalance where stitches occupy < 5% of the frame.
