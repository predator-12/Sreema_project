Tailor Photography Best Practices
Diffuse Lighting: Avoid heavy direct flash. Natural daylight or balanced workshop desk lamps yield highest segmentation contrast.
Flat Surface: Lay the garment panel flat on the cutting table without stretching or distorting the seamline.
Macro Close-up: Position smartphone camera 10–20 cm directly perpendicular to the questionable stitches.
Center The Seam: Keep the seam running vertically or horizontally across the inspection frame.
