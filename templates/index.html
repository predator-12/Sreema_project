How StitchSense Works

Transforming close-up fabric photos into precise tailoring alteration orders in under 3 seconds.

1. Capture Seam

Take a clear close-up photograph of the questionable seam using any smartphone or boutique camera under steady lighting.

2. Neural Segmentation

Our lightweight SMP U-Net evaluates the stitch structure, isolates skipped loops, tension ripples, and fabric holes at pixel precision.

3. Machine Remediation

Receive exact mechanical guidance: upper tension adjustments, needle point switch, presser foot calibration, and printable work tickets.
